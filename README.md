# CATIA CAD Modeling — Practice Projects

A collection of independent 3D CAD models created in **CATIA** to develop and demonstrate mechanical design, 3D modeling, and CAD skills.

## Models

### 1. Angled Bracket Assembly
Mounting base with four bolt holes and a curved connecting arm.

### 2. Machined Block Component
Rectangular machined block featuring two bore holes and a curved cutout.

### 3. Cylindrical Connector Part
Cylindrical connector component with two aligned bore holes.
